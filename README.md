WISAL

Overview

WISAL is a healthcare technology project focused on improving physiotherapy home exercise follow-up and patient adherence. The platform aims to connect patients, physiotherapists, and caregivers by providing a structured way to monitor exercise progress, track adherence, report difficulties, and support patients throughout their rehabilitation journey.

Problem Statement

Poor adherence to Home Exercise Programs (HEPs), with an estimated 30%–79% of patients discontinuing prescribed exercises after clinical consultations or hospital discharge, remains a major rehabilitation challenge. This is compounded by unclear exercise instructions, limited visual guidance, and inadequate safety information, which can lead to confusion and fear of re-injury. Meanwhile, therapists face significant administrative workloads when creating individualized exercise plans, and caregivers often lack the training and guidance needed to support patients safely, increasing stress and caregiving burden.

Proposed Features

Patient Support

* View assigned home exercise programs.
* Access exercise instructions and visual guidance.
* Log exercise adherence and completion.
* Report difficulties and reasons for missed exercises.
* Track progress over time.

Physiotherapist Support

* Create and manage individualized exercise programs.
* Monitor patient adherence and reported difficulties.
* Follow up on patient progress.
* Adjust exercise programs based on patient feedback.

Caregiver Support

* Access guidance to help patients perform exercises safely.
* Support patients in following their exercise programs.
* Monitor adherence and progress when needed.
